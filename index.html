What does a reversed voice sound like?

Speech played backwards sounds like a strange foreign language. Consonants that normally start with a sharp attack, like "t" or "k", fade in instead, and vowels glide the wrong way. That's why reverse speech is so fun to listen to, and so hard to imitate.

Why is reverse singing so hard?

To sing a song backwards you have to reproduce sounds your mouth never normally makes, in the right order and at the right pitch. The reverse audio challenge became popular on TikTok because even familiar songs come out hilariously wrong on the first try.

Frequently asked questions

How do I reverse my voice?

Press Record, allow microphone access, say or sing something and press Stop. Then press Play reversed to hear your voice backwards. You can change the speed and pitch and download the reversed audio as a WAV file.

What is the reverse singing challenge?

Record a line of a song, listen to it played backwards, then record yourself singing that backwards version. When your attempt is reversed again it should sound like the original song. The closer it sounds, the better you did.

Can I reverse an audio file online?

Yes. Choose Upload audio and pick an MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG or even a video file. It is reversed instantly in your browser and you can download the result.

Is my recording uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, reversing and downloading all happen locally in your browser. Your audio never leaves your device.

Is there a voice reverser app for iPhone?

Yes. Reverse Audio Recorder for iPhone and iPad lets you record and play sound backwards, reverse text, change speed and pitch, and save and share your recordings.

The microphone isn't working. What can I do?

Make sure you allowed microphone access when your browser asked. If you blocked it, click the lock or site-settings icon in the address bar and allow the microphone, then reload the page. Recording needs a secure (https) connection.
